Privacy Policy for Fight Thrill: Rise to Glory – VR Boxing

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information Fight Thrill: Rise to Glory – VR Boxing ("the game", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It covers both the single-player Arcade Mode and the online Multiplayer Mode.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected in all modes

  • Meta Account ID - Provided by the Meta Horizon platform to confirm you own the game and to enable platform features
  • Device Information - Hardware model, operating system version, and VR headset type
  • Gameplay Data - Session duration, achievements, in-game actions, and progression
  • Performance Data - Crash reports and performance metrics to improve stability

1.2 Additional information collected in Multiplayer Mode

  • Anonymous Player ID - When you open Multiplayer Mode, Unity Authentication creates an anonymous player ID for your device. It is used to connect you to matches, friends, and voice chat. It is not linked to your real name or email address.
  • Username and Profile Picture - The username you type and the profile picture you pick from the in-game list. These are shown to other players during matches, on friend lists, and in match invites.
  • Match Data - While you are in a match, your in-game position, head and hand movements, punches, health, and round results are sent to your opponent so the match can be played. This data is used only for the live match and is not stored after the match ends.
  • Friends Data - Your friend list, friend requests you send and receive, your online status (for example "In the lobby" or "In a match"), and match invites (which contain your username, profile picture, and a room code).
  • Voice Audio (Microphone) - Only if you allow microphone access. See Section 3.

We do not collect: Your real name, email address, physical location, contacts, photos, files, or payment information.

Tip: Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your in-game username. Your username is visible to other players.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain game functionality
  • To connect you with other players for online matches (Quick Match, room codes, and friend invites)
  • To show your username and profile picture to the players you play with
  • To let you add friends, see when they are online, and invite them to matches
  • To provide voice chat between you and your opponent during online matches
  • To improve gameplay experience and fix technical issues
  • To analyze player behavior for game optimization
  • To deliver software updates and new content

3. Microphone and Voice Chat

  • The game asks for microphone permission the first time you join an online match. Voice chat is optional.
  • If you allow it, your voice is sent in real time to your opponent during online matches using Unity Vivox.
  • We do not record or store your voice. Voice is only used for the live conversation.
  • You can mute your microphone at any time during a match (left controller X button, or the MIC button in the multiplayer menu).
  • You can deny or remove microphone permission at any time in your headset's settings. The game still works without it; only voice chat is turned off.
  • The microphone is not used in Arcade Mode.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the game:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. - Required for Horizon platform services and features (view Meta's policy)
  • Unity Technologies - Unity Gaming Services, used for (view Unity's policy):
    • Unity Authentication - anonymous player ID and username
    • Unity Multiplayer Services (Relay and Lobby) - creating and joining online matches
    • Unity Vivox - voice chat during online matches
    • Unity Friends - friend lists, friend requests, online status, and match invites
    • Unity Analytics - analytics and crash reporting
  • Other players - Players you are matched with can see your username, profile picture, and in-game movements, and can hear your voice if voice chat is on. Friends can see your online status.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Data Retention

  • Match data and voice audio - Used only during the live match and not stored.
  • Anonymous player ID, username, and friend list - Kept while you use the game, so your friends and profile are still there next time you play.
  • Gameplay, device, and performance data - Kept only as long as needed to improve the game and fix issues.
  • Your username and profile picture choice - Also saved on your headset so you don't have to set them again. Uninstalling the game removes this local copy.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Section 6).

6. Your Privacy Rights

You can:

  • Request access to, or deletion of, your data by emailing us at abdurrehman.mma12327@gmail.com. Please include your in-game username so we can find your data.
  • Change your username or profile picture at any time with the PROFILE button in Multiplayer Mode
  • Remove friends or decline friend requests in the FRIENDS menu
  • Turn off voice chat by muting your microphone or removing microphone permission in your headset settings
  • Play Arcade Mode only, which does not use online multiplayer, friends, or voice chat
  • Manage privacy settings through your Meta account
  • Opt-out of analytics through your device settings

We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

7. Data Security

Online matches, friends, and voice chat use Unity's services, which encrypt data sent over the internet. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the game means you accept the updated policy.
